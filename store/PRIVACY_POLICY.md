# Privacy Policy — Ducks in a Row

**Last updated:** 2026-07-04

Ducks in a Row ("the app") is a personal budgeting app built to work entirely
on your device. This policy is short because there isn't much to say: **we
don't collect your data, because we never see it.**

## Summary

- **100% offline.** The app has no backend server and makes no network
  requests to any service operated by us. There is nothing to phone home to.
- **No account, no sign-in.** You never create an account, log in, or link
  an identity (email, phone number, social login) to use the app.
- **No analytics, no ads, no trackers.** The app contains no third-party
  analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or crash-reporting services that
  transmit data off the device.
- **No data collection by the developer.** We do not receive, store, see,
  or have any access to your budgets, transactions, account balances,
  categories, income sources, or duck flock. All of it lives in a local
  database on your phone.

## What's stored, and where

Every piece of information you enter — accounts, transactions, envelopes,
income sources, carryover history, and your Duck System progress — is
stored in a local database on your device (SQLite, via Expo/React Native).
Nothing is uploaded, synced, or mirrored to any server. If you uninstall the
app or lose your device without a backup, that data is gone, the same as
deleting any other local file.

## App lock (PIN / biometric)

If you enable the optional app lock, your PIN is never stored in plain
text: it is salted and hashed on-device before being written to secure,
encrypted OS storage (Android Keystore–backed). Biometric unlock (fingerprint
/ face) is handled entirely by the operating system's own biometric APIs —
the app never receives your fingerprint or face data, only a yes/no result
from the OS.

## Notifications

Optional local reminders (bill due date, envelope-over-budget warning,
payday note) are scheduled **entirely on-device** using the operating
system's local notification scheduler. No notification content is sent to
or generated by a remote server, and no notification data leaves your
device. Each notification type is off by default and must be individually
enabled in Settings. On Android 13 and above, enabling notifications
requires the standard OS runtime permission prompt.

## Backups

The app includes an optional, **user-initiated** backup/export feature. When
you choose to export a backup, the app writes a single JSON file containing
your data (amounts as plain numbers, no other metadata about you) and hands
it to your device's standard share sheet — the same mechanism any app uses
to share a file. Where that file goes next (a folder, cloud storage like
Google Drive, email, a messaging app) is entirely your choice; the developer
never receives a copy. Restoring a backup happens the same way in reverse:
you pick a file from your device, and the app reads it locally.

## Permissions the app requests

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS` on Android 13+) | Only if you enable a reminder in Settings. |
| Biometric hardware (`USE_BIOMETRIC`) | Only if you enable biometric app lock. |
| Storage access (for sharing/reading a backup file) | Only when you tap "Export backup" or "Import backup." |

The app requests no location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS, or call-log
permissions.

## Children's privacy

The app is a general-purpose personal finance tool, is not directed at
children, and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any
age — because it does not collect information at all.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds a feature that changes any of the above
(for example, an optional cloud-sync feature), this policy will be updated
before that feature ships, and the change will be described in the app's
release notes.
