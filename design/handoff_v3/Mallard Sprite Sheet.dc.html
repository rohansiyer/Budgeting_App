Mallard sprite sheet

The new duck, built from the pixel reference. The grid grows from 14×12 to 16×14 to make room for legs and tail. Every animation is stepped, one discrete frame at a time, never interpolated; reduced-motion holds frame one. See it in context in the pattern mockups.

Legs on land
The last two grid rows are reserved for legs. They render in idle, waddle, walk-off and preen.
No legs in water
Swim frames drop the leg rows entirely; the body sits two rows lower and the waterline covers the bottom row.
Stepped only
Frame holds, no tweens. Idle keeps the 1150ms two-frame bob from the current sprite.
